Mathematical Methodology & Analysis Framework
1. Two-Stage Self-Reinforcing MC
Implements a double-nested feedback loop with simulation counts scaled to game odds:
Stage 1: Simulates $N$ draws from the base probability distribution.
Feedback Boost: Prominent survivors have their probabilities boosted using a reinforcement vector.
Stage 2: Simulates $N$ draws *only* from the survivors using boosted probabilities to find stable outputs.
2. Joint Pair Co-Occurrence
Evaluates pairs using joint discrete probability matrices:
P(A ∩ B) = Freq(A, B) / Total_Draws
Candidate combinations are scored based on the joint historical co-occurrence of all unique internal pairs.
3. Discrete Empirical Structures
Replaces standard Gaussian assumptions with empirical probability density fits for discrete variables:
Odd/Even Ratio & Spread: fit ∝ P_empirical(X)
Additionally, consecutive number persistence filters assess the probability of drawing consecutive pairings, matching physical machine characteristics.
